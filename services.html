Our Expertise

Bookkeeping & Accounting Services

We provide comprehensive financial management solutions tailored for small businesses, startups, and freelancers. Our approach combines meticulous double-entry bookkeeping with warm, personal support so you can focus on growing your business with confidence.

Monthly Bookkeeping

Stay on top of your finances with consistent, accurate monthly bookkeeping. We categorize transactions, reconcile accounts, and deliver clean financial statements every month so you always know where your business stands.

Includes bank feed setup, transaction categorization, monthly P&L and balance sheet reports, and proactive catch-up calls. Best for service-based businesses, consultants, and growing startups.

Account Reconciliation

Ensure every transaction in your books matches your bank and credit card statements. Our thorough reconciliation process catches discrepancies early, preventing costly errors and giving you peace of mind at tax time.

Includes monthly bank and credit card reconciliation, discrepancy investigation, adjusting journal entries, and a summary of unresolved items. Ideal for businesses processing high transaction volumes.

Financial Cleanup

Behind on your books? Our financial cleanup service brings order to messy records. We reconstruct your financial history, fix categorization errors, and set you up with a clean, audit-ready foundation going forward.

Includes historical transaction review, bulk recategorization, opening balance adjustments, and a handoff package with documented processes. Perfect for businesses catching up after a period of rapid growth or neglect.

Reporting & Insights

Turn your numbers into knowledge. We prepare custom financial reports and dashboards that help you understand profitability, cash flow trends, and growth opportunities — translated into plain language, not accounting jargon.

Includes monthly management reports, cash flow forecasting, budget-to-actual comparisons, and quarterly strategy check-ins. Suited for founders and small business owners who want data-driven decision support.

Bookkeeping clarity, answered honestly

We believe financial support should feel like a conversation, not a contract. Here are the questions we hear most often from small business owners and freelancers exploring bookkeeping services for the first time.

Who is SMN Bookkeeping for?

We work with small business owners, freelancers, and startup founders who need reliable double entry bookkeeping in accounting without the overhead of an in-house team. If you value clarity, approachable communication, and a partner who treats your numbers like their own, you'll feel right at home.

What does your bookkeeping support include?

Our small business bookkeeping service covers transaction categorization, bank and credit card reconciliations, monthly financial statements, and clean year-end reporting. We tailor the scope to your business — whether you need weekly catch-ups or a monthly deep-dive review — so you always have accurate records ready for tax time or investor conversations.

How often will I hear from you?

Communication rhythm is something we design together. Most clients receive a monthly summary with insights, but we're available between cycles for quick questions. Think of us as an extension of your team — present when you need us, never hovering when you don't.

Can we discuss catch-up bookkeeping?

Absolutely. Many of our relationships begin with a catch-up project — bringing past months or even years up to date before we transition into ongoing accounting services for small business. We'll assess the scope, give you a transparent timeline, and handle the backlog with care so you can start fresh.
